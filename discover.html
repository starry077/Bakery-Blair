Yellow Cheese Cream Filled

A journey balancing delicate sweetness and bold flavor! Succumb to this New York Roll featuring crisp, golden pastry generously filled with a creamy yellow cheese filling. Combining the richness of slightly salty cream cheese with a smooth, melting center, every bite is an explosion of flavor. Topped with a subtle glaze and a hint of savory garnish, this roll reimagines a New York classic with an original, comforting twist.

Tiramisu Cream Filled

Italian elegance meets New York flair. Discover this deliciously flaky New York Roll, filled with a creamy tiramisu filling. With intense coffee notes, smooth mascarpone, and a touch of cocoa, every bite evokes the famous Italian dessert—reimagined as chic street food. A perfect balance of crisp pastry and a melting center for a moment of pure indulgence.

Sweet Corn

A sweet surprise straight from the fields! This golden-pastry New York Roll conceals a smooth, velvety sweet corn cream—a unique flavor that subtly blends indulgence with lightness. The natural sweetness of the corn harmonizes perfectly with the crisp pastry, creating a taste experience that is unexpected yet incredibly addictive.
